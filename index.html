Fellowship

Gulf of Mexico Thermal Gradients Research

This project, High-Resolution Analysis of Gulf of Mexico Thermal Gradients (2020–2025) Using the MITgcm LLC2160 Dataset, examined ocean temperature patterns across coastal and offshore regions of the Gulf over five years. Using the MITgcm LLC2160 dataset, R, Jupyter, and the National Science Data Fabric (NSDF) through the Pelican platform, I analyzed large-scale ocean data to identify differences in surface temperatures. Offshore waters had a higher average surface potential temperature than coastal waters, which shows how the two regions respond differently to temperature change. The work earned me a NASA ARSET certification and strengthened my understanding of how ocean temperature patterns help researchers study hurricane intensification and coastal resilience.
